# A shifted square
<!-- [label{sec:square}] -->

This note demonstrates a small drafting workflow. Keep `file_name` literal.

> [!lemma] Nonnegative square
> <!-- [label{lem:square}] -->
> For real $x$ and $a$, the value $(x+a)^2$ is nonnegative.
>
> > [!proof]
> > The square of a real number is nonnegative.

> [!equation]
> <!-- [label{eq:square}] -->
> $$
> q_a(x)=(x+a)^2
> $$

Equation [ref{eq:square}] attains zero when $x=-a$, consistently with
[ref{lem:square}]. The label for this section is [ref{sec:square}].
